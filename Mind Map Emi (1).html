Concept Map: Theravāda Texts & Colonial Taxonomies

This interactive graph explores connections from the paper on Theravāda Buddhism and colonial frameworks.

How to Use:

  • Explore: Zoom (scroll), Pan (drag background).
  • Focus: Click a node to highlight connections & show details in the left sidebar. Click background to unfocus.
  • Details: Hover over nodes/edges for info (bottom-right panel).
  • Arrange: Drag nodes to position (they fix).
  • Adjust: Double-click a node to un-fix (allows brief adjustment).
  • Customize: Use the "Settings" tab for fonts, colors & legend.
